how it started

Tasnimul Hasan and Mahfuzur Rahman started Xenon Industries in 2024. They wanted to build cool products, the kind you'd actually want on your own desk.

And yes, "Industries" is a bit much. Blame Stark Industries.

There was no investor money. We bootstrapped Xenon from scratch with our own pocket money, then put every taka we made back into the next batch, again and again.

Today we're a team of six, all bachelor students. We work out of the FabLab, also called the Innovation Lab, at Daffodil International University, which supports us with the space.
