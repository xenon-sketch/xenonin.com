What is PikoBot?

A small desk robot. He shows expressions, displays short messages and runs a Pomodoro focus timer.

Does he listen to me or talk?

No. Piko has no voice and doesn't listen to conversations or collect personal data. He communicates with his face and short on-screen messages.

How do I start the focus timer?

Hold his head for 3 seconds. He runs 25 minutes of focus and a 5-minute break, with a soft chime at each switch. Hold again to stop.

Does he need Wi-Fi?

No. Faces, messages and the focus timer all work offline. Wi-Fi is only for the time and weather screen. To set it up, he opens his own hotspot and you pick your home network from your phone.

How big is he?

Palm-sized: about 5 cm wide and 6 cm tall on his stand.

Does he make sounds?

Only soft chimes, for example when a focus session or break starts. He has no voice.

Can I leave him on all day?

Yes. He uses very little power and is made to stay on your desk all day.

Is setup complicated?

Not at all. Plug him in, put him on your desk, and he's ready.
