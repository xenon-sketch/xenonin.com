how to claim

  1. Message us on WhatsApp

    Send a message to 01410-975319, or scan the QR code on the card in your box.

  2. Show us the problem

    We may ask for your order details and a photo or video of the issue.

    Approved claims get a brand-new replacement unit. We pay for the delivery.

what's covered

Covered for 6 months

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Functional issues
  • A new replacement unit at no extra product cost
  • Delivery for approved replacements, paid by us

Not covered

  • Damage from dropping or impact
  • Water or liquid exposure
  • Broken or cracked components
  • Unauthorized modification or repair
  • Misuse or improper handling

Warranty claims are handled by replacement only. We don't offer cash refunds.
